Website Planning Document

This is my plan for my Explore DR Congo website project.

1. Site Name

Explore DR Congo

I chose the name Explore DR Congo because my website is about the Democratic Republic of the Congo. I want the name to be simple and easy to understand. When people see the name, they can immediately know that the website is about discovering different parts of DR Congo.

Explore DR Congo

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of my website is to introduce people to the Democratic Republic of the Congo. I will share information about its geography, major cities, culture, languages, wildlife, natural resources, and interesting places to visit.

I will also use pictures and some interactive features to make the website more interesting and easier to explore.

3. Target Audience

My website is for people who want to learn more about DR Congo. It can be useful for students, travelers, people who are interested in African countries, and people who just want to discover more about the country.

4. Planned Website Pages

Home

The home page will give a general introduction to the Democratic Republic of the Congo. I will include information about the country, its geography, major cities, languages, and natural resources.

Culture & Wildlife

This page will talk about Congolese culture, languages, traditions, music, national parks, and some of the wildlife that can be found in DR Congo.

Places to Visit

This page will show some interesting places in DR Congo, such as Kinshasa, the Congo River, Virunga National Park, and Kahuzi-Biega National Park.

References

I will use this page to list the sources that I used for the information and pictures on my website.

5. Scenarios

These are some questions that someone visiting my website might have:

  • What are some interesting places to visit in DR Congo?
  • What languages do people speak in DR Congo?
  • What kind of wildlife can be found in DR Congo?
  • What are some of the major cities in the country?

6. Color Scheme

I chose green and yellow because I think these colors fit the subject of my website and give it a natural feeling. I will mainly use green for the header, navigation, headings, and some important parts of the website. I will use yellow for buttons, highlights, and small accents. I will use white for the main background so the content is easy to read.

I will use two fonts on my website. I chose them because I want the website to look clean and also be easy to read.

8. Planned Interactive Features

For the final website, I want to use JavaScript to make some parts of the website interactive. I want the interactive features to be useful instead of just adding JavaScript without a purpose.

  • Visitors will be able to filter information about places, wildlife, and culture.
  • Some information will be displayed dynamically using JavaScript.
  • Visitors will be able to save a place they are interested in using localStorage.
  • I will include a simple form where visitors can choose what they would like to learn more about.
  • JavaScript will respond to visitor actions such as clicking buttons and submitting the form.

9. Images and Responsive Design

I will use pictures of places, wildlife, cities, landscapes, and cultural activities from DR Congo. I will make sure the images are optimized for the website. I will also use lazy loading for appropriate images so the pages can load more efficiently.

I want the website to work well on phones, tablets, and computers. On a phone, the content will mostly be displayed in one column. On a larger screen, I will use multiple columns where it makes sense.
