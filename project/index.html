GEOGRAPHY & NATURE

Discover a land shaped by nature

DR Congo includes lowland rainforests, river networks, savannas, highlands, and volcanic landscapes. The Congo Basin is one of the world's most important tropical forest regions.

The Congo River and its tributaries are vital routes for transport, fishing, trade, and everyday life in many communities.
