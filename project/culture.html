Meet the traditions, creative energy, and incredible biodiversity of DR Congo.

A RICH CULTURAL HERITAGE

Many communities, one remarkable country

DR Congo is home to hundreds of ethnic communities and a wide variety of artistic, culinary, linguistic, and musical traditions. Cultural practices differ across regions and communities.

🗣️

Languages

French is the official language. Lingala, Swahili, Kikongo ya leta, and Tshiluba are recognized national languages. Many additional local languages are spoken.

🎵

Music & Dance

Congolese rumba is internationally influential. Soukous, gospel, contemporary music, and many local musical traditions are important parts of cultural life.

🍲

Food & Community

Common foods include cassava, plantains, maize, beans, fish, and leafy vegetables. Recipes and eating traditions vary across the country.

🎨

Art & Craft

Wood carving, sculpture, painting, textiles, pottery, and contemporary visual arts express diverse identities, histories, and creative ideas.

THE CONGO BASIN

A living natural treasure

The Congo Basin contains one of the world's largest tropical rainforest systems. Its forests store carbon, regulate water cycles, and provide habitats and resources for people and wildlife.

Protecting these environments requires conservation, responsible resource management, and respect for the communities who live in and depend on them.
